# 2026 AI Technology Trends

## 基本資訊

- 發布日期：2026-02-11
- 研究主題：AI 綜合動態 / General AI Updates
- 主要來源：https://www.ibm.com/cn-zh/think/news/ai-tech-trends-predictions-2026

## 概要

IBM's Peter Staar predicts that 2026 will be a turning point for AI research focus, with the industry shifting towards practical applications. Robotics and embodied AI will see rapid growth, while the scaling of large language models faces diminishing returns, prompting the industry to seek new technological breakthroughs.

## 核心價值

The focus of AI research is shifting from pure scale expansion to practical applications and technological breakthroughs.

## 應用情境與實務影響

Enterprises should allocate resources to AI projects with practical application value and invest in robotics and embodied AI development to avoid diminishing returns from over-reliance on large language models.
