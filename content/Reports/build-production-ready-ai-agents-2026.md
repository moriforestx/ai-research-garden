# Build production-ready AI agents in 2026 (w/out deleting your database)

## 基本資訊

- 發布日期：2026-03-05
- 研究主題：AI 應用與部署 / AI Applications & Deployment
- 主要來源：https://codingscape.com/blog/build-production-ready-ai-agents-in-2026-without-deleting-your-database

## 概要

A comprehensive guide to building production-ready AI agents in 2026, covering essential technologies like Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), Model Context Protocol (MCP), and multi-agent orchestration, with lessons from real-world failures and best practices for security and governance.

## 核心價值

Provides a practical blueprint for deploying AI agents safely and effectively in enterprise environments.

## 應用情境與實務影響

Helps organizations avoid common pitfalls and accelerate adoption of AI agents that deliver real workflow automation while maintaining control and compliance.

## 補充細節

The article outlines three core technologies making agents production-ready: RAG for secure data access, MCP as a universal agent-tool integration standard, and multi-agent orchestration for workflow automation. It emphasizes the importance of least-privilege access, hard stops, prompt injection defense, observability, and sandboxed environments. It also highlights real-world success stories from companies like Thomson Reuters, eSentire, L'Oréal, and Doctolib using AI agents for tasks ranging from legal research to threat analysis.
