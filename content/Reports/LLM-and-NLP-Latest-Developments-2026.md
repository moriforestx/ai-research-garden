## 概要

At the 2026 World Economic Forum, historian Yuval Noah Harari proposed that AI is creating a new language and humans are becoming 'observers'. He argued that while all language and thought once originated from the human brain, soon most text in our brains will come from machines. AI's role is to assemble text, symbols, and images to batch-produce new ideas, while humans become observers of this process. This is not only a technological innovation but also a shift in 'intellectual sovereignty'.
