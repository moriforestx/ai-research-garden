# MAI-Transcribe-1.5: Microsoft's Next-Generation Speech Recognition Model

## 基本資訊

- 發布日期：2026-06-02
- 研究主題：音訊與語音 / Audio & Speech
- 主要來源：https://www.timesofai.com/news/microsofts-mai-ai-models

## 概要

Microsoft announced MAI-Transcribe-1.5, an upgraded speech recognition model that improves accuracy and robustness, part of the MAI family of multimodal AI models unveiled ahead of Build 2026.

## 核心價值

Provides enhanced speech-to-text capabilities for diverse applications.

## 應用情境與實務影響

Enables more accurate transcription services, voice assistants, and accessibility tools.

## 補充細節

The model is part of Microsoft's broader update including MAI-Image-2.5 and MAI-Voice-2, aiming to strengthen its position in the AI market. It builds on the existing MAI-Transcribe-1 model.
