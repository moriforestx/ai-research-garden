# Efficiently Reconstructing Dynamic Scenes One D4RT at a Time

## 基本資訊

- 發布日期：2026-06-01
- 研究主題：電腦視覺 / Computer Vision
- 主要來源：https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Zhang_Efficiently_Reconstructing_Dynamic_Scenes_One_D4RT_at_a_Time_CVPR_2026_paper.html

## 概要

This paper introduces D4RT, a feedforward transformer network that efficiently reconstructs the geometry and motion of dynamic 4D scenes from video by jointly inferring depth, spatio-temporal correspondence, and camera parameters, enabling lightweight and scalable training and inference.

## 核心價值

Introduces D4RT, a novel method for efficient 4D scene representation that outperforms prior approaches across diverse benchmarks.

## 應用情境與實務影響

Enables real-time dynamic scene understanding for robotics, autonomous vehicles, AR/VR, and video editing applications.

## 補充細節

D4RT avoids costly per-frame decoders by using a unified decoding interface to query any point in space and time. Evaluations show new state-of-the-art performance on 4D reconstruction tasks. The authors are from Google DeepMind, UCL, and Oxford.
