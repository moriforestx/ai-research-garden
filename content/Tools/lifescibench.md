# LifeSciBench: A Benchmark for Research-Grade Life-Science Tasks

## 基本資訊

- 發布日期：2026-06-18
- 研究主題：大型語言模型與自然語言處理 / LLM & NLP
- 主要來源：https://openai.com/index/introducing-life-sci-bench/

## 概要

OpenAI introduced LifeSciBench, a benchmark with 750 expert-authored tasks and over 1,000 supporting artifacts to evaluate AI systems on realistic life-science research workflows, including evidence handling, design, optimization, validation, translation, and scientific communication.

## 核心價值

Provides a comprehensive, realistic evaluation framework for life-science AI applications.

## 應用情境與實務影響

Enables more meaningful assessment of AI models in biotech and pharma R&D, guiding development toward practically useful systems.

## 補充細節

LifeSciBench moves beyond quiz-style biology questions to simulate real-world research processes, helping bridge the gap between model capabilities and actual scientific utility.
